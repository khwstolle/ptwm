PTWM ships several plane codecs. The compressor trial-encodes each plane
with every codec in the configured menu and keeps the smallest result —
selection is per-plane, and no codec is the default.

## Available Codecs

PTWM includes multiple entropy coders, dictionary coders, and specialized predictors.
A detailed overview of each available codec, its optimal use-cases, and its theory of operation can be found in the [Codecs](codecs/) section.

## Dispatch

Each plane gets its own codec dispatch record in the `.ptwm`
[container](/concepts/container). The decoder reads the dispatch
record, allocates the per-codec state, and runs the inverse codec.
